Estimated shipping cost

Estimate only — not a live carrier quote. Based on published USPS Ground Advantage retail rates (mid-2026) and an approximate zone derived from ZIP prefixes, not a real carrier zone chart. Actual cost depends on carrier, service level, account type, and current surcharges.

Estimated shipping insurance

Estimate only — based on the USPS Notice 123 domestic insurance fee schedule (2026). The first $100 of declared value ships insured for free on Priority Mail, Priority Mail Express, and Ground Advantage. USPS raised these rates twice in 2026, so treat this as directional, not a live quote — and note standard coverage caps at $5,000; higher values need Registered Mail or third-party insurance.
