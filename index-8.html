Prototype assumptions — replace with real data before shipping this

  • Cushion thickness uses a simplified ideal-energy-absorption formula (thickness = drop height ÷ G-limit, then divided by an assumed 0.3 real-foam efficiency factor, loosely anchored to the one worked example in the spec) — not the manufacturer cushion curves from Section 6. Treat output as directional, not engineering-final. G-limit defaults to the midpoint of the selected tier and is editable.
  • Drop height is the real ISTA 1A/3A weight-based table from the spec (≤75lb → 30", 75–100lb → 24", 100–150lb → 18"; above 150lb is flagged as freight territory).
  • Box selection picks the smallest box from a short standard-size list whose sorted dimensions clear the item + cushioning. A real build should use the full carrier-preset list from the spec.
  • Cost figures (box $/volume, foam $/volume, tape/labor flat fee) are placeholder rates for testing the shape of the model, not sourced vendor pricing.
  • CO2e figures use the material factors from the spec (virgin vs. recycled cardboard and LDPE) applied to estimated material weight. Transport emissions are not included in this prototype.
  • Object identification now checks a real 1,230-item master database (1,000 items from user-provided product data with programmatic tier/tag assignment, plus 27 hand-curated items filling categories a mall-directory audit flagged as missing — jewelry, eyewear, health & beauty, grocery, apparel, shoes & sports, toys & games) before falling back to a coarse aspect-ratio guess. A match auto-fills weight, dimensions (marked "est." until you click-measure over them), fragility tier, and hazard tags with a visible reasoning note. No barcode or real vision/OCR recognition is wired in yet, per the spec.
  • Measurement assumes a straight-on photo; angled photos will distort results, per the spec's known limitation.
